The Classic Granny Square

★☆☆

30 min · 4 mm hook · DK cotton, 2 colours

You'll need

Step by step
  1. Make a magic ring. Ch 3 (counts as your first dc), work 2 dc into the ring, ch 2. *3 dc, ch 2* three more times to make four corners. Join with a sl st to the top of the starting ch-3.
  2. Round 2: sl st across to the first ch-2 corner space. Ch 3, then (2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in the same space. Ch 1. In each remaining corner work (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) followed by ch 1. Join.
  3. Round 3: sl st to the corner space. Work the corner as before. Ch 1, then work 3 dc into the ch-1 space along the side, ch 1. Corner, ch 1, side, ch 1, and repeat all the way around. Join.
  4. Keep adding rounds, working one extra 3-dc group per side each time, until your square is the size you want. Fasten off and weave in the ends.

Tip: Swap colours every round for a scrappy, cheerful look.

Little Toadstool

★★☆

2 hours · 2.5 mm hook · DK cotton, red + cream

You'll need

Step by step
  1. Cap. In red, magic ring with 6 sc. R2: inc in every stitch (12). R3: [sc, inc] ×6 (18). R4: [2 sc, inc] ×6 (24). R5: [3 sc, inc] ×6 (30).
  2. R6–R9: sc around (30). R10: [3 sc, dec] ×6 (24). Fasten off. Embroider small white dots with a yarn needle.
  3. Stem. In cream, magic ring with 6 sc. R2: inc ×6 (12). R3–R7: sc around (12). Stuff lightly.
  4. Stuff the cap, sew the stem underneath and add a smile between rounds 4 and 5 of the stem.

Tip: Keep your stitches snug so the stuffing can't peek through.

Five-Petal Flower

★☆☆

20 min · 3.5 mm hook · DK cotton, 2 colours

You'll need

Step by step
  1. Make a magic ring. Ch 1, work 10 sc into the ring and join with a sl st. Pull the ring tight.
  2. Petals: *in the next st work (sl st, ch 2, 3 dc, ch 2, sl st), then sl st in the next st.* Repeat 5 times for five petals.
  3. Fasten off. Sew a button, bead or a few French knots in the centre.

Tip: Sew it onto a bag, a hat, a jumper cuff or a birthday card.

Cozy Ear-Warmer Headband

★☆☆

3 hours · 5 mm hook · Chunky wool blend

You'll need

Step by step
  1. Ch 16. Row 1: hdc in the 3rd ch from the hook and in each ch across (14). Ch 2, turn.
  2. Row 2 onward: hdc in each stitch across. Ch 2, turn. Keep going until the band wraps around your head with a little stretch, about 50 cm for most adults.
  3. Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Seam the short ends together with a whip stitch, then weave in the ends.
  4. Optional: twist the band once before seaming for a cute knot at the front.

Tip: Crochet a few more rows for wider ear coverage.

Amigurumi

Jelly Keychain

★★☆

2.5 hours · 2.5 mm hook · Cotton 4-ply, pink + white

You'll need

Step by step
  1. Bell. Magic ring with 6 sc. R2: inc ×6 (12). R3: [sc, inc] ×6 (18). R4–R6: sc around (18). R7: [sc, dec] ×6 (12). Stuff.
  2. Add a ruffle: work 2 dc in each stitch around the front loops of R6 for a frilly skirt.
  3. Tentacles. Attach yarn at the bottom edge. Ch 25, sc into the 2nd ch from the hook and in every ch to the end, sl st into the base. Repeat 6 times.
  4. Sew the keyring to the top of the bell.

Tip: Use variegated yarn for tentacles that look like they're glowing.
