Our little story

Val’s Yarntopia is a crochet shop for people who love soft, wonky, handmade things. We started with a few balls of yarn and a lot of enthusiasm, and now it's a friendly corner for anyone who likes making with their hands.

Alongside the yarn, we share small monthly challenges, simple patterns and a gallery of what everyone makes. No pressure, no perfect stitches.
