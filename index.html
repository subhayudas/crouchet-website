Small shop, big heart

Val’s Yarntopia is a small-batch crochet shop for anyone who loves soft, slightly wonky, completely handmade things. Each hat, bag and tiny critter is made to order in cotton yarns picked for colour and cuddliness.

We also share free patterns and a monthly challenge, because the best part of crochet is making something with your own two hands.
