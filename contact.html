Good to know

Are pieces ready to ship?

Most pieces are made to order, so they take a little while: about 3 days for small blooms up to 2–3 weeks for bags and big wearables. We'll confirm timing when you order.

Can I choose my own colours?

Yes, please do. Tell us your favourite colours and we'll suggest yarns from our stash.

How do I care for crochet?

Hand wash gently in cool water, squeeze out the water without wringing, and dry flat. Keep amigurumi away from tumble dryers.

Do you ship?

Shipping options and costs are confirmed when you get in touch, based on where you live.

Can I take part in the challenge without buying?

Absolutely. The monthly challenge and patterns are free for everyone.
